- Add `estimate` and `unit` to a progress line's settings in `@loomcli/prompts`. With `estimate: true`, a running line also shows its rate and time left after the elapsed time, `◓ Copying files ▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱▱▱ 40/120 · 12.0s · 3.3 files/s · about 24s left`, computed from the counts you report through `update()` over the last 10 seconds and counting down between reports, with no timer on your work. Neither part shows until a second of reports has passed, and neither shows on an end line or where the line prints plain lines. On a narrow row the rate gives way first. See [Rate and time left](docs/libs/prompts.md#rate-and-time-left).
- Add optional `estimate`, `rate`, `remaining`, and `unit` to `ProgressLine`, the progress view's data. Every line supplies them whether or not `estimate` is set, so a view override can draw a rate or a time left of its own, and data you build by hand for `out.draw` draws without them.
