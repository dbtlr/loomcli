- Add `out.region()`, which opens a live region at the bottom of stderr and returns its handle at once, with `draw`, `end`, `signal`, `report`, `live`, and `closedBy`. Core keeps every open region below every other write, in the order the regions opened, and redraws them after each write. It erases a region whole after a terminal resize rewraps it, holds the other regions still while an input region is open, and draws no region without input under `-q`. It closes a region left open when its opener finishes, before a failure report, and on cancellation, and aborts the region's signal first. `@loomcli/core` exports `Region`, `RegionOptions`, `RegionCloser`, `ProgressReport`, and `ProgressEvent`. The [core reference](docs/core.md#live-region) describes the region.
- Add `progress(context, { label, total? })` to `@loomcli/prompts`. It draws a spinner, or a bar with counts, in its own live region, and its handle has `update`, `end`, `fail`, and `track`. Off a terminal, under `-q`, and under an invocation by name, it writes one start line and one end line through the semantic lanes. The optional `progressStyle()` plugin sets every line's spinner, in the cli-spinners shape, and its bar cells, and `@loomcli/prompts/views` exports the `progressLine` view. The [prompts reference](docs/prompts.md#progress) describes the lines.
- Change `@loomcli/prompts` questions to draw in input regions. A lane message written while a question asks lands above its frame, a frame that a terminal rewrapped after a resize is erased whole, and two questions asked at once take turns. A question whose region core closes for a failure, such as work beside it in `Promise.all` that rejects, or for the return of what asked it, draws its cancelled line, leaves raw mode, shows the cursor, and rejects, so stdin no longer holds the process open after the run.
