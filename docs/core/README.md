# Core reference

The [principles](../principles.md) rank above this reference, and a rule here that conflicts with one is reworked through a superseding decision record.

The 0.15.0 rescope is recorded in [ADR-0093](../decisions/0093-a-value-that-bypasses-the-types-gets-no-rule.md) through [ADR-0102](../decisions/0102-dotenv-imports-every-variable-the-process-has-not-set.md). Where a rule below states what one of them decides, the code keeps enforcing the earlier rule until that record's implementing phase ships, and the error rows and acceptance lists for a removed rule stay here until that phase removes them with the code.

Core resolves marked strings under a destination-aware [rendering policy](styles.md). The [view registry](views.md) is implemented under accepted ADR-0021: the package exports `view`, `override`, `lanes`, `View`, and `ViewContext`, and the retired `failures`, `renderFailure`, `FailureRenderer`, `Renderer`, and `RendererContext` are gone. The named [Loom theme](../plugins/theme.md) and explicit color fallbacks are implemented under accepted ADR-0022 and ADR-0029. The results lane under [Results](results.md) is implemented under accepted ADR-0023: `result()`, `rows()`, and `views()` are authoring calls, `out.results` is on every channel, and the package exports `RowView`, `DeclaredRowView`, `ResultError`, and `incompleteResult`. The [formatter](../plugins/format.md), the `onCommandAttach` [lifecycle hook](lifecycle-hooks.md) with its exported `AttachedCommand`, `CommandAttachHook`, and `ResultView` types, and the [middleware](middleware.md) context's `request`, typed by the exported `Request`, and `view` are implemented under accepted ADR-0028, and the invocation order in [Invocation](invocation.md) describes the chain behind local parsing. The [table](../plugins/pack-views.md#table) and [records](../plugins/pack-views.md#records) pack views are implemented under the 2026-09-17 entries in ADR-0008 and ADR-0023. [Collecting extensions](extensions.md#collecting-extensions), the `extensions` a [lifecycle hook](lifecycle-hooks.md) reads, and [help's values in the manifest](../plugins/help.md#help-in-the-manifest) are implemented under accepted ADR-0031, and the [manifest](../plugins/manifest.md) plugin is implemented under its contract, installed by both example applications. [Accepted values](../plugins/help.md#accepted-values) on help rows, `accepts`, and `helpArgument` are implemented, and the formatter's description names only its default. [Input sources](input-sources.md), the environment binding and the configuration source, are implemented under accepted ADR-0032: core exports `SourceResolver`, `SourceContext`, and `SourceAnswer`, `inspect()` and the manifest publish `env`, and textstat binds `--min-bytes` and `--total` to variables. [Help variants](../plugins/help.md#help-variants), the middleware context's `spellings` typed by the exported `PluginOptionSpellings`, and `HelpPage.variant` are implemented under accepted ADR-0040: `-h` prints the compact page and `--help` the extended page. [Declared exit codes](failures.md#declared-exit-codes) are implemented under accepted ADR-0045: core exports `FailureExitCode` and the fifteen `sysexits.h` constants, `LoomError`'s constructor takes the message and no code, and jsonkit's `PathNotFoundError` exits 65. The [failure view context](failure-views.md#failure-view-context), the `onFailure` [failure hints](lifecycle-hooks.md#failure-hints), and the issue fields core keeps under [Issues and validator failures](validation.md#issues-and-validator-failures) are implemented under accepted ADR-0046: the package exports `FailureViewContext`, `FailureView`, `FailureHook`, and `FailureHookContext`, and the private `@loom/explain` plugin adds its hint to both example applications' unknown-option diagnostics. The [failure message](failures.md) rules and [issue codes](../libs/validators.md#issue-codes) are implemented under accepted ADR-0047 and ADR-0048: core's default text opens every usage failure with the application name, routing candidates leave out deprecated children, `escapeControlCharacters` escapes the bidirectional controls and marks, every sentence the [audit](failures.md#9-audit) lists ends with its fix, and `@loomcli/validators` exports `issueCode` and the catalog's codes. The [suggestions](../plugins/suggestions.md) plugin and [help's failure hint](../plugins/suggestions.md#helps-failure-hint) are implemented under their contract: the pack exports `@loomcli/plugins/suggestions`, `help()` points every usage error at its page, and both example applications install both. [Translators](failures.md#translators) are implemented under accepted ADR-0049: core exports `translate`, `Translation`, `Translator`, and `ErrorClass`, `LoomError`, `FatalError`, `InputError`, and `DeclarationError` accept the platform's `ErrorOptions`, and jsonkit's reader holds no `JSON.parse` catch, so a malformed document exits 65. The manifest's [declared failures](../plugins/manifest.md#manifest-failures) are implemented under their contract: `manifestCommand` takes `failures`, `--manifest` lists each Command's failures and joins their codes to core's rows in `exitCodes`, and jsonkit declares `invalid-json`, which its translator raises with code 65, and `path-not-found`. [Development builds](release-facts.md#development-builds) and the [Developer Diagnostic](declaration-faults.md#developer-diagnostics) for defects are implemented under accepted ADR-0050 and ADR-0051: core exports `diagnosticRule`, `DiagnosticRule`, `Finding`, and `DiagnosticParts`, `DeclarationError` and `InternalError` take a rule beside their earlier constructors, `Host` gains `readSource`, and `@loomcli/loom` is published beside the libraries. [Release facts](release-facts.md) replace the packet ADR-0050 introduced, under [ADR-0065](../decisions/0065-release-facts-on-the-host-say-how-the-running-application-was-built.md), so a run reads its build from `host.release`. Every declaration fault in core, the pack, and the catalog carries its rule's descriptor and explanation, and findings wherever a call declared it, under the identities [Developer Diagnostics](declaration-faults.md#developer-diagnostics) lists, and a development build runs the converter check of [Input schema](validation.md#input-schema). The [identity convention](plugin-contract.md#identity-and-installation) is implemented under [ADR-0096](../decisions/0096-the-identity-grammar-is-a-naming-convention.md): `plugin()`, `extension()`, and `view()` take any string as an identity, and core exports `isRuleIdentity`. The configuration plugin's one-file lookup, TOML and YAML files, and its `file` and `short` settings are implemented under accepted [ADR-0054](../decisions/0054-the-configuration-plugin-reads-one-file-per-run-found-by-the-authors-file-pattern.md): `@loomcli/plugins` depends on `yaml` and `smol-toml`, and textstat reads `.textstat.{toml,json}` with `-c` as the short spelling of `--config`. Every `Invalid declaration: ` and `Internal error: ` line this reference shows outside those sections quotes the fault's `sentence`; `run()` renders the fault by build as [Development builds](release-facts.md#development-builds) states, and never with either prefix. The [manpage](../plugins/manpage.md) plugin is implemented under its contract and proposed ADR-0079: the pack exports `@loomcli/plugins/manpage` with `manpage`, `refreshManpages`, and the `RefreshContext` the skills plugin exports, the installed-files module creates a plugin's missing parent directories with the mode it names, and jsonkit installs the plugin. The [list](../plugins/pack-views.md#list), [heading](../plugins/pack-views.md#heading), [rule](../plugins/pack-views.md#rule), [prose](../plugins/pack-views.md#prose), and [box](../plugins/pack-views.md#box) pack views are implemented under proposed ADR-0088: the pack exports `@loomcli/plugins/list`, `@loomcli/plugins/heading`, `@loomcli/plugins/rule`, `@loomcli/plugins/prose`, and `@loomcli/plugins/box`, and jsonkit's `doctor` reports its host checks through them. The [color choice](color.md) and the [color plugin](../plugins/color.md) are implemented under proposed [ADR-0089](../decisions/0089-an-operators-color-or-no-color-settles-the-runs-color-right-after-host-capture.md): core reads the color-marked option's spellings from argv right after host capture and rejects a misplaced mark under `@loomcli/core/color-mark`, `inspect()` and the manifest publish `color` on each Boolean option, the pack exports `@loomcli/plugins/color`, and jsonkit installs it. The [verbosity](verbosity.md) and the [verbose plugin](../plugins/verbose.md) are implemented under proposed [ADR-0090](../decisions/0090-an-operators-v-or-q-settles-the-runs-verbosity-right-after-graph-build.md): `out` carries `debug` and `trace` through the `@loomcli/core/lanes/debug` and `@loomcli/core/lanes/trace` views, core settles the run's verbosity right after graph build with the parser's own word reader and rejects a misplaced mark under `@loomcli/core/verbose-mark` and `@loomcli/core/quiet-mark`, `inspect()` and the manifest publish `verbose` and `quiet` on each counted option, the pack exports `@loomcli/plugins/verbose`, and jsonkit installs it and writes its `Reading doc.json.` line through `out.debug`.
